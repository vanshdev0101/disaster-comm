Gateway wiring: ESP32 + Ra-02 (8 wires + capacitor)

3.3 V only. The radio goes to 3V3, never VIN or 5V. Power the board only after checking every wire against this page.

1. Connections (this is what to wire)

Capacitor 100-470 µF: long leg (+) to Ra-02 3.3V, stripe leg (-) to Ra-02 GND (the second GND pin, next to 3.3V).
Antenna: u.FL plug clicked onto the socket, whip screwed on, before power.

4. Check before plugging in USB

  • Red wire goes from ESP32 3V3 to Ra-02 3.3V. Black goes from ESP32 GND to a Ra-02 GND. Do not swap these two.
  • Orange, yellow, green, blue go to NSS, SCK, MISO, MOSI. Check MISO and MOSI are not swapped.
  • White goes to RST and purple to DIO0, on the left strip.
  • Nothing is on VIN or 5V.
  • Expected result after flashing the gateway firmware: serial monitor prints LoRa ready.
